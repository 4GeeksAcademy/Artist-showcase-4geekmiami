About Me

I'm Will, a singer-songwriter born and raised in Miami, Florida. I grew up between my grandmother's salsa records and my older brother's soul mixtapes, and my music lives right where those two worlds meet.

I write songs about late nights, long drives down Biscayne Boulevard, and the people who make a city feel like home.

Frequently asked questions

What genre is Will's music?

A blend of soul, indie pop, and Latin pop, with live horns and hand percussion.

Where is Will from?

Miami, Florida, USA.

How many albums has Will released?

Three: First Light (2019), Biscayne Nights (2022), and Neon Tide (2025).

Career

From open-mic nights to sold-out rooms — the milestones so far.

  1. 2016

    First open mic

    Played three original songs at a Little Havana café and never looked back.

  2. May 2019

    Debut album First Light

    Self-released and recorded in a converted garage studio.

  3. March 2022

    Biscayne Nights

    Lead single "Causeway" passed 10 million streams.

  4. April 2024

    Festival debut

    First festival main-stage set in front of 20,000 people.

  5. September 2025

    Third album Neon Tide

    Debuted in the top 10 of the independent album charts.
